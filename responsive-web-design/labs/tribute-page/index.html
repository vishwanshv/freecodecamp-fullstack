Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India and the People's President

A Life of Inspiration

  • 1931 - Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a modest family.
  • 1954 - Graduated in physics from Saint Joseph's College in Tiruchirappalli.
  • 1958 - Joined the Aeronautical Development Establishment of the Defence Research and Development Organisation.
  • 1962 - Joined India's space program and became involved in the development of launch vehicles.
  • 1980 - Played a major role in the successful launch of the Rohini satellite using the SLV-III rocket.
  • 1981 - Received the Padma Bhushan for his contributions to science and engineering.
  • 1990 - Received the Padma Vibhushan for his distinguished service to the nation.
  • 1997 - Awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour.
  • 2002 - Became the 11th President of India and served until 2007.
  • 2015 - Passed away while delivering a lecture to students at IIM Shillong.
"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."

Dr. Kalam's life continues to inspire people to dream boldly, work with determination, and contribute to the progress of society.
